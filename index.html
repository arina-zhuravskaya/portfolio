Concept

Daheim is a small Polish brand of handmade furniture and home objects. The brief: build a visual identity disciplined enough to scale across a brandbook, a catalog, packaging, and stickers, without losing the warmth and craft the brand is selling.

Process

The unlock was treating a single continuous looping line as a system element rather than decoration. One shape, applied with different weights and contexts: it carries section numbers in the catalog, becomes the wave frame on candle labels, appears as a sticker motif, and sets the rhythm of the brandbook.

Defended at PJAIT Warsaw, BA New Media Arts, 2025. GRADE 5+
