Current Pharma Data viz Design system Feb 2026 →

Translating clinical data across 7 EMEA markets.

As Graphic Designer & Production Associate at Greenpark Digital — embedded at AstraZeneca's Warsaw office — I turn complex medical data into decks, charts, and HCP-facing surfaces.

What I did

Designed client-facing & educational decks. Translated medical data into hierarchy-led visualisations. Migrated website visuals into AZ Poland's HCP portal within the existing design system. Delivered print materials & Knak email campaigns for campaigns across 7 markets.

Why it matters

Pharma communication has a high cost of being misread. The job isn't to make charts pretty — it's to remove every ambiguity between a clinical insight and the person who has to act on it.

Co-creator Children's podcast Identity Motion Audio Nov 2024 — Jan 2025

Belarusian fairy tales for kids in the diaspora.

A children's podcast bringing classic Belarusian fairy tales to children and parents living abroad. I co-created the project — designing identity & motion, recording & editing audio.

What I did

Co-created the show from the ground up — visual identity, character treatments, episode covers, animated intros. Recorded and edited audio episodes through classic Belarusian fairy tales for a generation growing up outside Belarus.

Why it matters

A children's identity has to charm the kid and earn the parent's trust at once. Diaspora families are a quiet, underserved audience — the brief was to make Belarusian feel like home, not like homework.

05 / 06
Podcast Mental health Social system Video edit Jun — Sep 2023

A visual language for hard conversations.

A Belarusian podcast on mental health, social issues & relationships. I designed the show's visual system — posts, reels, engagement content — and edited podcast video for cross-platform distribution.

What I did

Built a custom Figma design system to keep posts, reels, and engagement content visually coherent across a fast-moving editorial calendar. Edited podcast video for both vertical (Reels / TikTok) and horizontal (YouTube) distribution.

Why it matters

Mental-health conversation deserves a visual register that's neither clinical nor cute. We wanted something that looked like a friend speaking honestly — not a brochure.

06 / 06
Identity Posters Cultural initiative Belarusian Oct 2022 — Jun 2023

An identity for a quieter kind of gathering.

Zrabi Ciszej ("Make It Quieter") — a Belarusian language-and-music platform hosting musical gatherings. I designed the identity, posters, social content, and the guidelines holding physical and digital together.

What I did

Built the visual identity around a typographic system that could carry posters, t-shirts, social posts, and venue signage with the same rhythm. Wrote guidelines that kept physical and digital coherent across nine months of events.

Why it matters

"Make it quieter" wasn't a tagline — it was a posture. The visuals had to feel intimate without becoming precious; rooted in Belarusian culture without becoming nostalgic.
